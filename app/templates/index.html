Meet Line Dancing John

Hi, I'm Line Dancing John. John or LD is fine! Line dancing entered my life in 2016 when someone invited me to try a lesson. I instantly fell in love with the music, the energy, and the welcoming community.

Although my professional background is in social work, I became passionate about teaching line dancing. Within just a few months I was helping friends and coworkers learn, and eventually I was invited to teach my own classes.

Today I teach classes, private lessons, parties, celebrations, business events, and even cruise ship groups. My goal is simple: make line dancing fun, welcoming, and easy for everyone.

I'm not classically trained, and I think that's my biggest strength. I teach from the perspective of someone who learned from the ground up, making every lesson approachable for beginners while keeping it enjoyable for experienced dancers.
